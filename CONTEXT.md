# Dealership inventory

The dealership's vehicle stock and the advertisements used to market it.

## Language

**Vehicle**:
A physical car, distinct from its photo folders and advertisements. Two cars of the same make, model, and year remain separate vehicles.

**Source folder**:
A folder containing material associated with a vehicle. Multiple source folders can refer to the same vehicle, and a folder name alone does not establish identity.

**Availability**:
The dealership's confirmed position on a vehicle: unknown, in stock, reserved, sold, or archived. Archived means retained as history without asserting that a sale occurred.

**Listing**:
An advertisement for a vehicle on one channel, with its own publication status. A missing or expired listing does not establish that the vehicle sold.

**Observation**:
A dated piece of evidence about a vehicle or listing from a folder, registration document, marketplace, or person. An observation can be uncertain or conflict with another observation.

**Publication approval**:
An explicit decision to publish a particular version of a listing to selected channels. Changes to that version require a new approval.

**Mulkiya**:
A Qatar vehicle registration card used as evidence for vehicle details. It can contain personal information and is separate from public listing photos.
